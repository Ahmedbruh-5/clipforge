## Want more free music?

Download free tracks yourself and use **Upload music** in the ClipForge editor:

- [Mixkit Free Stock Music](https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/) — royalty-free for YouTube/social (attribution appreciated)
- [Pixabay Music](https://pixabay.com/music/) — free for commercial use under Pixabay Content License
- [YouTube Audio Library](https://studio.youtube.com/channel/UC/music) — free tracks marked for reuse
- [Internet Archive — Free Music Archive](https://archive.org/details/freemusicarchive) — filter by public domain / CC0

Always check the license on the download page before uploading.
